Introduction / ਭੂਮਿਕਾ

Director Colonization Punjab

The Colonization Department has played a vital role in establishing New Grain Markets and Townships across Punjab, providing planned commercial spaces and modern market facilities for farmers, traders, shopkeepers, and the general public.

The Department has acquired land at 156 locations and has sold 21,557 plots in 91 mandis up to 31.12.2025, generating revenue of ₹650.30 crore. A total of 4,847 plots are yet to be auctioned.

The Department derives its revenue through the open auction of plots and recovery of installments against plots already sold in various New Mandi Townships. An income target of ₹3.00 crore has been fixed for the year 2025-26.
